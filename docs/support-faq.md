# Support FAQ

Answers to the questions support gets most often about invoices and reminders. Written by support, checked by the billing team.

**A customer paid but still got a reminder.** Payments are recorded when the bank transfer arrives. A transfer made the day before the reminder may not be in yet. Ask the customer to ignore the reminder and check the payment in the back office.

**When does the first reminder come?** Seven days after the due date, at about 6 in the morning. See [reminders.md](reminders.md) for all levels.
